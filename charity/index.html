Charitable Purposes

The primary object of the Trust is to promote, support, develop and manage community amateur sport, recreational facilities and healthy active lifestyles across the MetroPark domain and the wider Hibiscus Coast community.

This includes looking after administrative coordination, securing grant distributions for protective gear and equipment updates, and driving collaborative multi-sport facility updates for local families, clubs, and youth sports teams.
