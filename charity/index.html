Charitable Purposes

The MetroPark Community Sports Charitable Trust is explicitly set up to foster, promote, and develop sustainable community sports facilities, public pavilions, and associated recreational assets at Metro Park (Millwater). Our constitutional structure ensures that operations remain non-profit, keeping access costs affordable for regional sporting codes, families, and youth groups.
