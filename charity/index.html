Charitable Purposes

The MetroPark Community Sports Charitable Trust is explicitly set up to foster, promote, and develop sustainable community sports facilities, public pavilions, and associated recreational assets at Metro Park (Millwater). Our constitutional structure ensures that operations remain non-profit, keeping access costs affordable for regional sporting codes, families, and youth groups.

Governance & Asset Control

Club-Led Representation

The board structure explicitly integrates representative oversight from active sports clubs utilizing the park fields daily, matching resources to practical needs.

Perpetual Asset Lock

Constitutional provisions ensure that all funds raised or infrastructure built remain securely preserved entirely for community sport utility within the region.
