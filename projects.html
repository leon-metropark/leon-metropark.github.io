Projects

Metro Park development

The Trust's focus is practical, community-led infrastructure that supports multi-sport participation at Metro Park.

Pavilion and shared facilities

The Trust continues to advocate for shared facilities that support local clubs, participants, families and the wider community. Key priorities include changing rooms, storage, meeting and social spaces, and practical amenities that make the park more usable for community sport.

2013

Early involvement

Community sport representatives began working toward improved facilities and long-term planning for Metro Park.

2017

Working group engagement

Local sporting organisations continued to participate in working group discussions about the future of Metro Park facilities.

2019

Pavilion concept publicly reported

The sports pavilion concept was reported publicly, outlining proposed amenities and the role of the Trust in supporting a shared facility.

2024

Trustee representation updated

Updated club representation strengthened the Trust's connection to local sporting codes and community users.
