About Us

The MetroPark Community Sports Charitable Trust represents sporting organisations and community groups connected to Metro Park in Millwater.

Our purpose is to advocate for and develop community-focused sporting infrastructure that supports participation, wellbeing and long-term growth.

Our Vision

Our ongoing focus is to secure practical, sustainable and appropriate infrastructure at Metro Park.

👥

Clubrooms

A shared home base where sports codes can host community meetings, coordinate fixtures and celebrate local milestones.

🏠

Amenities

Changing facilities, storage zones and accessible public spaces that support daily park operations.

🌱

Sustainable Design

A practical layout that respects site constraints, maximises utility and manages long-term community cost.
