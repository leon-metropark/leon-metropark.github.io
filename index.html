About Us

The MetroPark Community Sports Charitable Trust represents the sporting organisations and community groups that utilise Metro Park in Millwater. Our purpose is to advocate for and develop community-focused sporting infrastructure that supports participation, wellbeing and long-term growth.

Through collaboration, planning and investment, the Trust works to ensure Metro Park remains a vibrant sporting and recreation hub for current and future generations.
