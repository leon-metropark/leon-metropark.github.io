About Us

The MetroPark Community Sports Charitable Trust is built directly by trust members and local club representatives utilizing Metro Park in Millwater. Our deep-rooted commitment is ensuring that local sports teams, fitness enthusiasts, and neighborhood families have accessible infrastructure tailored specifically to community scale and needs.

We believe in collaborative, affordable, and sustainable infrastructure developments that allow grass-roots sports to thrive in the Hibiscus Coast region.

Our Core Principles

  • ✔ Community First: Infrastructure directly guided by the local clubs who use the fields daily.
  • ✔ Affordability: Ensuring entry costs to sports remain low for young families.
  • ✔ Sustainability: Building a practical, durable multi-use space that handles growth responsibly.
