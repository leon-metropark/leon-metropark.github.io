About the Trust

Supporting community sport at Metro Park.

MetroPark Community Sports Charitable Trust brings local sporting codes and community leaders together to support shared facilities, inclusive participation and long-term sport and recreation outcomes for the Hibiscus Coast.

Our purpose

MetroPark Community Sports Charitable Trust supports the development, management and long-term success of community-focused sports facilities and programmes at Metro Park. Our trustees bring experience from local clubs, community sport, governance, business, disability services, facility planning and volunteer leadership.

Jeff Olufson

Trustee

Jeff Olufson represents the Hibiscus Coast Cricket Club, of which he is a life member. A founding trustee of MCSCT, Jeff’s involvement with Metro Park dates back to 2013. He brings decades of community sport experience to the Trust, with a long record of service across the Hibiscus Coast contributing to club development, youth participation and collaborative facility planning. Jeff is committed to strengthening local sport through sound governance, strategic investment and accessible opportunities for families across the region.

Ray Finch

Trustee

Ray Finch represents Hibiscus Hockey and brings a strong background in sport, health, disability advocacy and senior leadership. A father of three and regular sports parent, Ray is the CEO of Laura Fergusson Trust. He holds a Master’s in Health Sciences and has extensive sports injury and clinical experience. Before immigrating to New Zealand in 2015, Ray worked in high-performance sport, including director-level roles with international cricket and hockey teams. Since arriving in New Zealand, he has led disability charities and advocated for the rights of disabled people. His love of sport and community drives his contribution to MCSCT.

Scott Beard

Trustee

Scott Beard has been involved in football for more than 50 years as a player, coach and administrator. He served as President of HBCAFC for 12 years and has been a trustee of MCSCT since its inception. Scott’s passion is seeing children involved in sport — any sport — and helping create opportunities for young people to participate, develop and enjoy being part of their local sporting community.

Leon Smith

Trustee

Leon Smith is the owner-director of Zettabit, an IT consultancy company he has operated for more than 15 years. Alongside his professional background in technology, systems and project delivery, Leon has a strong record of volunteering in community sport. His experience includes governance, fundraising, sponsorship, digital communications, strategy, asset management, website management, team coordination and coaching development. As a trustee of MCSCT, Leon contributes practical business, technology and community sport experience to support inclusive participation and sustainable facility development at Metro Park.
