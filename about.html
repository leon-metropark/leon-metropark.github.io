Who We Are

The MetroPark Community Sports Charitable Trust coordinates community amateur sports infrastructure and organizational governance within the Auckland region. Our mission centers on providing robust, accessible physical environments where regional clubs can focus completely on coaching, youth participation, and game-day competition.
